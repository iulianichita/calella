Why calella exists

A contest, an admission exam or a recruitment campaign is never a single post. It is about the announcement, the calendar, registrations opening, a reminder before each stage, the results, the final announcement and a thank-you.

Organizations usually plan this by hand. Someone designs each graphic, someone checks the dates, and someone rewrites every caption for LinkedIn, Instagram and X. When a date moves, the whole chain needs to be redone.

Calella takes the dates you already know and turns them into the whole series, ready to publish.

Weeks of small tasks

How it works

Four steps, from connecting your account to a finished schedule.

  1. Connect Buffer

    You sign in on Buffer's own page and approve access. Calella never sees your password. Your Buffer channels then appear in your dashboard.

  2. Describe your event or campaign

    Give it a name and a short description, pick the campaign type, and enter the dates. Add hashtags if you want them on every post.

  3. Choose a poster and your channels

    Pick one of the graphic styles, or publish text only. A live preview shows how the event will look. Then choose the channels the campaign should go to.

  4. Create and schedule everything

    Calella writes the posts, generates a graphic for each one and schedules them in Buffer. You get a list showing what was scheduled, for which channel and on which date.

Two types of campaign

Choose the one that matches how your event is organized.

Single event

One day that matters

You enter the event day. You can add an advance announcement on a date you choose, and a thank-you post afterwards. If you leave the thank-you date empty, it goes out 7 days after the event.

Multiple stages

A contest in several rounds

You start with two stages and can add as many as you need. Each stage has a name and a date, and calella builds the posts around them.

One campaign, every channel

You write nothing per platform. Calella adapts each post to the channel it is going to, and you choose the channels per campaign from the ones you connected in Buffer.

The full text, followed by your hashtags. Published at 11:30.

The full text, followed by your hashtags. Published at 18:00.

A worked example

Imagine a university running a two-stage admission contest. These dates are only an illustration.

  1. Early March Awareness What the contest is, who can apply and where to find the calendar.
  2. Mid March Stage 1: registrations Registration for contest opens.
  3. Late March Stage 1: results Who moved on to the next round.
  4. April Stage 2: final interviews Meeting the professors.
  5. May Final announcement Final results.

With calella, the person organizing the contest fills in one form with these dates instead of building each post separately.

Good to know

Publishing time

Each post goes out at the time that works best on its platform: X at 14:00, Instagram at 18:00, LinkedIn at 11:30, Facebook at 18:00, and 12:00 on any other channel. All times are in UTC.

Graphics are loaded at publishing time

Buffer downloads each graphic from calella when the post goes out, so calella needs to be online on that day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Buffer account?

Yes. Calella schedules through Buffer, so you need an account with at least one channel connected there.

Can calella see my Buffer password?

No. You sign in on Buffer's own page, and calella only receives permission to act on your behalf.

What is calella allowed to do in my account?

Create posts, read your posts, and read your account details and channels. You can see the exact permissions on Buffer's approval screen before you accept.

Which platforms can I post to?

Any channel you have connected in Buffer. You choose which ones to use for each campaign.

Do I have to design the graphics?

No. Choose a graphic style, and calella adds the campaign name, the post title and the date for each post. You can also choose text only.

Why was a post skipped?

Its date had already passed when you created the campaign. Check the dates and try again.
